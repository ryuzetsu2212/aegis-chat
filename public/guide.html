1. Tentang AegisChat

AegisChat adalah aplikasi chat real-time dengan enkripsi ujung-ke-ujung (E2EE) yang dikelola sepenuhnya manual oleh pengguna, mirip komunikasi PGP, tapi dalam bentuk antarmuka chat.

Prinsip utama: server tidak tahu isi pesan Anda. Server hanya menerima, meneruskan, dan menyimpan ciphertext (teks acak hasil enkripsi). Kunci rahasia tidak pernah dikirim ke server.

2. Registrasi Akun & Verifikasi OTP

  1. Buka halaman depan aplikasi (/); Anda akan disambung form registrasi.
  2. Isi Username, Email, dan Password (minimal 6 karakter).
  3. Pilih avatar emoji sebagai identitas visual akun Anda.
  4. Klik tombol registrasi. Sistem mengirim kode OTP 6 digit ke email Anda.
  5. Buka email, salin kode OTP, masukkan di halaman verifikasi.
  6. Klik verifikasi. Akun aktif dan bisa dipakai login.

⚠️ Kode OTP berlaku 10 menit dan hanya bisa dipakai sekali. Kalau kadaluarsa, daftar ulang atau minta kode baru.

3. Login

  1. Buka /login.
  2. Masukkan Username atau Email dan Password.
  3. Klik LOGIN. Berhasil → langsung diarahkan ke halaman Chat.

Sudah login sebelumnya? Buka /login lagi, Anda otomatis dialihkan ke halaman chat (sesi disimpan di browser).

4. Lupa Password

  1. Di halaman login, klik Forgot Password?.
  2. Masukkan email akun Anda.
  3. Cek email: kode OTP 6 digit akan dikirim.
  4. Masukkan OTP, lalu tentukan password baru (min. 6 karakter).
  5. Login dengan password baru.

5. Kontak

  1. Di navbar atas, klik Contacts. Jendela daftar kontak terbuka.
  2. Gunakan kolom pencarian untuk menyaring nama kontak.
  3. Klik salah satu kontak untuk membuka percakapan dengannya.
  4. Nama partner chat di header area obrolan juga bisa diklik, membuka halaman profilnya.

Status bar di bawah navbar menampilkan waktu lokal Anda dan username yang sedang login.

6. Enkripsi Pesan (Manual)

Inilah alur wajib sebelum mengirim pesan:

Langkah 1Tulis pesan pada kolom input paling kiri.
Langkah 2Masukkan Secret Key (kunci rahasia) yang sudah Anda sepakati dengan lawan bicara. Kunci ini tidak pernah dikirim ke server.
Langkah 3Klik ENCRYPT. Pesan berubah menjadi ciphertext acak di kolom input.
Langkah 4Klik tombol ▶ untuk mengirim ciphertext tersebut.

Algoritma enkripsi: AES-256 (CryptoJS), diproses 100% di browser Anda. Kolom Secret Key otomatis kosong setelah mengirim.

7. Dekripsi Pesan

  1. Pesan masuk tampil sebagai ciphertext acak, normal dan memang disengaja.
  2. Klik tombol DECRYPT pada balon pesan yang ingin dibaca.
  3. Masukkan Secret Key yang sama dengan milik pengirim.
  4. Pesan asli muncul menggantikan ciphertext.

⚠️ Salah kunci = dekripsi gagal. Pastikan Anda dan lawan bicara memakai Secret Key yang persis sama (termasuk huruf besar/kecil). Sepakati kunci lewat kanal lain (pertemuan langsung, telepon, dll), jangan lewat chat ini sendiri.

8. Kirim Gambar / File

  1. Klik ikon 📁 di samping kolom input.
  2. Pilih gambar. Nama file dan ukurannya tampil sebagai pratinjau placeholder.
  3. Jangan lupa isi Secret Key, lalu klik ▶ untuk mengirim.
  4. Gambar tampil di percakapan dan bisa dibuka oleh penerima.

9. Tanda Pesan Dibaca

  • ✓, pesan terkirim, belum dibaca penerima.
  • ✓✓, pesan sudah dibaca penerima.

10. Hapus Pesan

Hapus satu pesan: arahkan ke balon pesan milik Anda sendiri, klik ikon 🗑️. Pesan langsung hilang.

Hapus massal (bulk delete):

  1. Klik tombol Select di header area chat. Checkbox muncul di setiap pesan Anda yang masih bisa dihapus.
  2. Centang semua pesan yang ingin dihapus.
  3. Klik Delete (n) untuk menghapus sekaligus, atau Cancel untuk batal.
  4. Setelah selesai, tombol kembali ke Select.

⚠️ Aturan 24 jam: pesan hanya bisa dihapus dalam rentang kurang dari 24 jam sejak dikirim, dan hanya oleh pengirimnya.

11. Profil & Pengaturan Akun

Buka lewat Profile di navbar, atau klik nama partner chat.

  • Lihat profil: avatar besar, nama, dan @username.
  • Ganti avatar: klik avatar besar Anda → pilih emoji baru → simpan.
  • Edit profil (tombol ✏️ Edit Profile):
    • Ganti username saja → langsung tersimpan, tanpa OTP.
    • Ganti email dan/atau password → wajib konfirmasi OTP 6 digit yang dikirim ke email Anda. Langkah keamanan ekstra agar orang lain tak bisa merubah kredensial akun Anda.

Tombol ← Back mengembalikan Anda ke area chat.

12. Blokir Pengguna

  1. Buka profil pengguna lain (klik namanya di header chat, atau lewat Contacts).
  2. Klik tombol blokir di halaman profilnya.
  3. Setelah diblokir: Anda berdua tidak saling tampak di daftar kontak dan tidak bisa saling berkirim pesan.
  4. Blokir bisa dibuka kembali (unblock) dari tempat yang sama.

Contoh status yang mungkin tampil: "Blocked by you", "Has blocked you", "User Blocked You".

13. Catatan Keamanan Penting

  • Zero-knowledge server: server hanya menyimpan ciphertext. Isi pesan, Secret Key, dan hasil dekripsi tidak pernah menyentuh server.
  • Secret Key = tanggung jawab Anda. Kunci hilang → pesan tak bisa dibaca siapa pun. Bagikan kunci hanya ke orang terpercaya, lewat kanal di luar aplikasi ini.
  • Password akun disimpan sebagai hash bcrypt di server; tetap jangan pakai password yang sama dengan layanan lain.
  • OTP via email melindungi perubahan email/password dan proses reset. Jangan bagikan kode OTP ke siapa pun.
  • Pesan terenkripsi aman tersimpan di server, tapi kekuatan enkripsi bergantung pada Secret Key Anda, pakai kunci panjang dan sulit ditebak.

14. Menambahkan Kontak & Permintaan Pertemanan

  1. Buka Contacts → klik Add Contact.
  2. Ketik username yang ingin ditambahkan; hasil pencarian muncul langsung.
  3. Klik nama orangnya untuk mengirim permintaan pertemanan.
  4. Yang dikirim melihat permintaan itu di menu Contacts, bagian Friend Requests, dan bisa menekan Accept (terima) atau Reject (tolak).
  5. Begitu diterima, kalian saling muncul di daftar kontak dan bisa mulai chat.

Menambahkan kontak selalu butuh persetujuan orangnya — tidak ada yang bisa memaksa masuk daftar kontak Anda.

15. Grup Chat

Membuat grup:

  1. Buka Contacts → New Group.
  2. Isi nama grup, centang kontak yang mau dimasukkan, lalu klik Create. Anggota terpilih langsung masuk grup.

Chat grup: grup tampil di daftar Groups pada menu Contacts. Klik grup untuk membukanya. Enkripsinya sama persis dengan chat pribadi: tulis pesan → isi Secret Key → ENCRYPT → ▶. Grup terakhir yang Anda buka diingat — refresh halaman tetap kembali ke grup yang sama.

Group Info: klik nama grup di header obrolan. Di sana ada nama grup, jumlah anggota, dan daftar anggota — pembuat grup bertanda (admin).

Hak admin:

  • Tambah Anggota — pilih kontak untuk diundang. Yang diundang tidak langsung masuk: undangan muncul di Contacts miliknya, bagian Group Invites, dan ia harus menekan Join (setuju) atau Decline (tolak). Masuk grup selalu atas persetujuan orangnya sendiri.
  • Keluarkan anggota — tombol hapus di samping anggota non-admin pada daftar anggota.
  • Bubarkan Grup — membubarkan grup dan menghapus seluruh keanggotaannya. Hanya admin.

⚠️ Keluar Grup meminta konfirmasi dan tidak bisa dibatalkan — untuk bergabung lagi, admin harus mengundang Anda ulang. Kalau yang keluar adalah admin, ia harus memilih pengganti admin dari anggota yang tersisa.

1. About AegisChat

AegisChat is a real-time chat application with end-to-end encryption (E2EE) that is fully manual and user-controlled, similar to PGP communication, but packaged in a chat interface.

Core principle: the server never knows what you say. It only receives, routes, and stores ciphertext (random-looking encrypted text). Your secret key is never sent to the server.

2. Registration & OTP Verification

  1. Open the app's front page (/); you'll land on the registration form.
  2. Fill in Username, Email, and Password (minimum 6 characters).
  3. Pick an emoji avatar as your account's visual identity.
  4. Submit the form. A 6-digit OTP code will be emailed to you.
  5. Open your email, copy the OTP, enter it on the verification page.
  6. Confirm. Your account is now active and ready to log in.

⚠️ The OTP expires after 10 minutes and can only be used once. If expired, register again or request a new code.

3. Login

  1. Go to /login.
  2. Enter your username or email and password.
  3. Click LOGIN. On success you're redirected straight to the Chat page.

Already logged in before? Reopening /login automatically redirects you to the chat page (session stored in your browser).

4. Forgot Password

  1. On the login page, click Forgot Password?.
  2. Enter your account's email address.
  3. Check your inbox: a 6-digit OTP will be sent.
  4. Enter the OTP, then set a new password (min. 6 characters).
  5. Log in with your new password.

5. Contacts

  1. In the top navbar, click Contacts. A contact list window opens.
  2. Use the search box to filter contacts by name.
  3. Click a contact to open a conversation with them.
  4. The chat partner's name in the conversation header is also clickable, it opens their profile page.

The status bar under the navbar shows your local time and the logged-in username.

6. Encrypting Messages (Manual)

This is the required flow before sending any message:

Step 1Type your message in the leftmost input field.
Step 2Enter the Secret Key you agreed on with your chat partner. This key is never sent to the server.
Step 3Click ENCRYPT. Your message turns into random-looking ciphertext in the input field.
Step 4Click the ▶ button to send that ciphertext.

Encryption algorithm: AES-256 (CryptoJS), processed 100% in your browser. The Secret Key field clears automatically after sending.

7. Decrypting Messages

  1. Incoming messages appear as random ciphertext, normal and intentional.
  2. Click the DECRYPT button on the message bubble you want to read.
  3. Enter the same Secret Key the sender used.
  4. The original message replaces the ciphertext.

⚠️ Wrong key = decryption fails. Make sure both sides use the exact same Secret Key (including capitalization). Agree on keys through another channel (in person, phone call, etc.), never through this chat itself.

8. Sending Images / Files

  1. Click the 📁 icon next to the input field.
  2. Choose an image. Its name and size appear as a placeholder preview.
  3. Don't forget the Secret Key, then click ▶ to send.
  4. The image appears in the conversation and can be viewed by the recipient.

9. Read Receipts

  • ✓, message delivered, not yet read.
  • ✓✓, message has been read.

10. Deleting Messages

Delete a single message: hover over your own message bubble and click the 🗑️ icon. It disappears instantly.

Bulk delete:

  1. Click Select in the chat header. Checkboxes appear on each of your deletable messages.
  2. Tick every message you want removed.
  3. Click Delete (n) to remove them all at once, or Cancel to abort.
  4. When done, the button reverts to Select.

⚠️ 24-hour rule: messages can only be deleted within less than 24 hours of being sent, and only by the sender.

11. Profile & Account Settings

Open via Profile in the navbar, or click a chat partner's name.

  • View profile: large avatar, display name, and @username.
  • Change avatar: click your large avatar → pick a new emoji → save.
  • Edit profile (✏️ Edit Profile button):
    • Username-only change → saved instantly, no OTP needed.
    • Email and/or password change → requires a 6-digit OTP sent to your email. This extra safeguard prevents others from changing your credentials.

The ← Back button returns you to the chat area.

12. Blocking Users

  1. Open another user's profile (click their name in the chat header, or via Contacts).
  2. Click the block button on their profile page.
  3. Once blocked: neither of you appears in each other's contact list, and messaging between you is disabled.
  4. You can unblock later from the same place.

Example statuses you may see: "Blocked by you", "Has blocked you", "User Blocked You".

13. Important Security Notes

  • Zero-knowledge server: only ciphertext is stored. Message contents, the Secret Key, and decrypted results never touch the server.
  • The Secret Key is your responsibility. Lose it and nobody can ever read those messages again. Share keys only with trusted parties, outside this app.
  • Your account password is stored as a bcrypt hash server-side; still, don't reuse passwords from other services.
  • Email OTP protects password/email changes and resets. Never share OTP codes with anyone.
  • Encrypted messages are safe at rest on the server, but encryption strength depends on your Secret Key, use long, hard-to-guess keys.

14. Adding Contacts & Friend Requests

  1. Open Contacts → click Add Contact.
  2. Type the username you want to add; matching results appear instantly.
  3. Click their name to send a friend request.
  4. The recipient sees it under Contacts → Friend Requests and can press Accept or Reject.
  5. Once accepted, you both appear in each other's contact list and can start chatting.

Adding a contact always requires that person's approval — nobody can force their way into your contact list.

15. Group Chat

Creating a group:

  1. Open Contacts → New Group.
  2. Enter a group name, tick the contacts to include, then click Create. Selected members join immediately.

Group chat: groups appear in the Groups list inside the Contacts menu. Click a group to open it. Encryption works exactly like 1-on-1 chats: type your message → enter the Secret Key → ENCRYPT → ▶. Your last opened group is remembered — refreshing the page returns you to the same group.

Group Info: click the group name in the chat header. It shows the group name, member count, and the member list — the creator is tagged (admin).

Admin powers:

  • Tambah Anggota (Add member) — pick a contact to invite. They do not join right away: the invite appears in their Contacts under Group Invites, and they must press Join (accept) or Decline. Joining a group always requires that person's consent.
  • Remove member — the delete icon next to non-admin members in the member list.
  • Bubarkan Grup (Dissolve) — dissolves the group and removes all memberships. Admin only.

⚠️ Leave group asks for confirmation and cannot be undone — to rejoin, an admin must invite you again. If the admin is the one leaving, they must first pick an admin successor from the remaining members.
